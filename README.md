# ☕  COFFEE SHOP SALES ANALYSIS
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of the sales data for the Maven Roasters coffee shop, covering the period from January to June 2023.
#  📊  PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analyses the coffee shop sales data of maven roaster coffee shop with the aim of structuring the data set, identifying sales trend, product performance, and customer purchasing pattern. The tools used in this project includes Microsoft excel for data cleaning , data validation and  building of interactive dashboard, pivot table and pivot chart for visualization. this project demonstrates how data analytics can support business decision making.  

# 🎯 BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

1.	Prepare and transform the raw sales dataset into a clean, structured, and analysis-ready format by cleaning the dataset and creating relevant calculated fields.

2. Evaluate the overall sales performance of Maven Roasters by analyzing total revenue and transaction volume during the six-month reporting period. 

3.	Identify monthly sales trends to determine periods of peak and low business performance.

4.  Examine sales performance across different product categories to identify the products that contribute most significantly to business revenue.
  
5.	Analyze customer purchasing patterns by evaluating sales performance across different days of the week and hours of the day in order to identify peak trading periods and periods of low customer activity.
	
6.	Develop an interactive dashboard that effectively communicates key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, and business insights in a clear and visually engaging manner.
   
7.	Generate actionable insights and evidence-based recommendations that support improvements in inventory management, workforce scheduling, marketing strategies, and overall operational efficiency.

# 🛠️ TOOLS & TECHNIQUES

TOOLS: Microsoft Excel, Pivot Table, Pivot Chart.

TECHNIQUES: Data cleaning, Data Transformation, Data Validation, KPI Analysis, Data Visualization and Dashboard Development.

# 🧹 DATA PREPARATION

The raw dataset was cleaned and prepared using Excel. The process included checking for missing values, checking for duplicate entries, correcting data types, checking for invalid values or numeric fields, checking for inconsistencies in the text field and preparing the dataset for analysis.

# 📊 ANALYSIS AND DASHBOARD

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to monitor key performance indicators and provide a visual overview of revenue generated, number of transactions carried out, product performances and sales performance.

# 💡 KEY INSIGHTS

1. Total revenue across all three branches for the period was $698,812.33 from 149,116 transactions, an average revenue of $4.69 per transaction.
 
2.	Coffee is the dominant category, generating $269,952.45 (38.6% of total revenue), followed by Tea at $196,405.95 (28.1%).
	
3.	Revenue grew steadily month over month, from $81,677.74 in January to $166,485.88 in June, June alone accounts for 23.8% of the half-year's total revenue, suggesting either strong seasonal growth, business expansion, or a growing customer base over the period.
	
4. Trading is heavily concentrated in the morning: the 7–11 AM window alone accounts for roughly 52% of daily revenue, with 10 AM the single busiest hour ($88,673.39, 12.7% of total revenue). Trade falls sharply after 6 PM and is minimal by 8 PM.
 
5.	Monday is the highest-revenue weekday ($113,845.83), closely followed by Sunday ($111,287.46); Saturday is the lowest ($88,498.67), a pattern that runs counter to the common assumption that weekends are busiest for cafés, and is worth verifying against store hours or promotions.
	
# 🎯 BUSINESS RECOMMENDATION

1.	Align staffing levels with the observed demand curve: schedule the strongest staffing coverage for the 7–11 AM window (over half of daily revenue) and consider leaner coverage after 6 PM, when trade drops sharply.
	
2.	Investigate the Monday/Sunday-high, Saturday-low weekday pattern before making changes — confirm whether it reflects genuine customer behavior, store hours, local footfall (e.g., office workers on weekdays), or a data artefact, since it runs against the common assumption that cafés peak on weekends.
   
3.	Use the strong and growing performance of Coffee and Tea (66.7% of revenue combined) to guide inventory prioritization and promotional focus, while monitoring whether smaller categories (e.g., Packaged Chocolate, Flavors) are worth the shelf space and holding cost they occupy.
   
4.	Treat the steady month-on-month revenue growth from January to June as encouraging but preliminary, and re-examine it once a full 12 months of data are available.
   
5.	Extend the data collection period to a full calendar year (and ideally multiple years) to allow genuine seasonal comparison rather than a single half-year snapshot.
